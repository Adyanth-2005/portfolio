01
Healthcare Systems · Flagship

HIS — Hospital Information System

A full-stack platform unifying patient records, clinical workflows and ML-assisted decision support — polyglot persistence behind a FastAPI gateway, with a hybrid RAG engine serving citation-checked, hallucination-guarded clinical answers.

FastAPIPostgreSQLElasticsearchMilvusLlama 3.1 / OllamaDockerFHIR R4
Problem

Hospital data lives in silos — records, documents, search and retrieval each in different systems, none of them conversational.

Approach

Polyglot persistence: PostgreSQL (EHR + RBAC), Elasticsearch (BM25), Milvus (dense vectors), MinIO (documents). Hybrid RAG with reciprocal-rank fusion over a locally hosted Llama 3.1.

Result

Citation-checked, hallucination-guarded clinical Q&A with FHIR R4 interoperability, early-warning vitals scoring, and Docker/Nginx deployment via Cloudflare Tunnel.

02

Multi-Agent Medical Severity Classification

A multi-agent LLM pipeline classifying patient symptom descriptions into Mild / Moderate / Severe / Critical tiers with BioMistral-7B & MedAlpaca-13B — reaching 92% accuracy on held-out data.

BioMistral-7BMedAlpaca-13BQLoRAPyTorchHugging Face
Problem

Free-text symptom descriptions are ambiguous; mis-triage of severe cases is the costliest failure mode.

Approach

4-bit QLoRA fine-tune of BioMistral-7B (r=16, α=32); a confidence-aware hard-flag override that escalates safety-critical cases; a ClinicalReasoningAgent for structured explanations.

Result

92% classification accuracy on held-out data, with explainable severity calls and guaranteed escalation on hard flags.

03

NexusGuard — AI Governance Platform

A hackathon AI-governance platform with Need-Role-Level access control — role-based query classification returning GRANTED / DENIED / BLOCKED, backed by a RAG pipeline over 150 synthetic policy documents.

Llama 3.1 8BChromaDBFastAPIOllamaSpring BootReact
Problem

Corporate LLM assistants leak information across roles — access control needs to live inside the AI layer, not around it.

Approach

Owned LLM fine-tuning + the FastAPI layer. Built the ChromaDB RAG pipeline; diagnosed overfitting in the fine-tuned model and pivoted to an Ollama system-prompt deployment.

Result

Working NRL access control demo at hackathon pace — an honest engineering pivot that shipped over a fragile fine-tune.

04

Quantum Kernel ECG Classification

A QML model for ECG disease classification using quantum kernels + QSVM, with a novel physiology-aware quantum feature map and multi-lead entanglement — improving robustness on noisy, low-data ECG.

QiskitQSVMQuantum Kernelsscikit-learn
Problem

Classical ECG classifiers degrade fast on noisy signals and small labeled datasets — exactly where clinics live.

Approach

Quantum kernels + QSVM with a physiology-aware feature map: multi-lead entanglement encodes inter-lead relationships directly into the circuit.

Result

Improved accuracy and robustness over classical baselines in noisy, low-data regimes.
